Company Overview

Crossroads Infrastructure Inc. operates as a heavy civil engineering contractor serving Greensboro, North Carolina, and surrounding areas. We manage comprehensive concrete construction, roadway realignments, and storm drainage networks for NCDOT and various municipalities.

Founded in 2020, we've built our reputation the old-fashioned way: showing up, doing the work right, and keeping our crews and clients on schedule. We're a tight-knit team — small enough to know every job by name, experienced enough to handle the region's toughest roadway and site work.
